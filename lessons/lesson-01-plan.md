# Lesson 1 plan: Python foundations

Student: Vineet h. True beginner, healthcare and data work, goal is Python for his career and his weekly spreadsheet and report tasks.
Format: live lesson, student types everything, teacher guides. Notebook: lesson-01-python-foundations.ipynb

Note: this lesson was first taught live on Fri Oct 23, 2026 and went well. The notebook rebuilds it in Colab so Vineet can rerun and practice every line, and it becomes Lesson 1 of the course for every future student.

## Shape

0 to 5 min, welcome and the one rule: predict before you run, every time.
5 to 15 min, print and variables, on his own name and a patient example.
15 to 25 min, numbers, lists, sum and len, average patient age.
25 to 35 min, for loops and if, filtering patients 50 and over.
35 to 45 min, records as dictionaries, counting visits by condition.
45 to 50 min, challenges preview and the micro task: bring three repeat spreadsheet or report tasks from work.

## The win to protect

He runs the counting by category block himself and can say in his own words what the loop does.

## Delivery notes

Speak slowly, define every new word the first time it appears. He types, you guide. Protect the records and counting block if time gets tight, drop the challenges walkthrough instead.
